## Security checklist
- Dedicated mailbox, unique password, 2FA with authenticator, recovery codes stored privately
- No passwords, OTPs, Telegram bot token, or recovery codes in repository or chat
- Separate brand accounts and business contact details
- Check platform rules and Belgian/EU disclosure and financial-promotion obligations
- Verify all outbound links and handles before publication

## Editorial guardrails
- Educational content first; dated sources for factual claims
- No unverified prices, invented results, guaranteed profits, fake scarcity, or undisclosed paid promotion
- Show risks, conflicts of interest, and corrections
- Review legal obligations before paid trade recommendations or VIP signals
